# NPP Storm Damage Campaign

This package contains the final campaign video exports, six static ads, social copy variations, the landing-page/CRM requirements draft, and the official NPP logo asset.

## Video exports

- `npp_storm_ad_vertical_720p.mp4` — 9:16, 720 × 1280, 15 seconds.
- `npp_storm_ad_4x5_720p.mp4` — 4:5, 720 × 900, 15 seconds; full vertical footage preserved with softened side fill.
- `npp_storm_ad_vertical_1080x1920_rescaled.mp4` — 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 15 seconds; Lanczos rescale from the 720p master. The larger dimensions do not add source detail.

## Static and campaign documents

Static ads are provided in both 4:5 and 1:1 folders. Campaign Docs contains the proposed ad copy/captions and the draft form-to-GoHighLevel requirements/checklist. Brand Assets contains the official logo used in the final creative.

## Launch dependency

The supplied storm-damage landing page identifies its form as a visual demo that does not transmit or store submissions. Complete the CRM connection and consent/privacy review, then pass the end-to-end checklist before directing paid traffic to that page.
