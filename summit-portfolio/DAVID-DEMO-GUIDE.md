## Recommended walkthrough

1. Start on the campaign showcase and explain that the three campaign angles are separate entry points for different homeowner intents.
2. Play each video and open the static ad thumbnails. Explain that a homeowner clicking an ad goes to the matching campaign landing page.
3. Open the Storm Damage page first and walk through the form as a homeowner.
4. Demonstrate a qualified path using a residential property, the actual homeowner, a ZIP code within NPP's approved combined service-area ZIP list, no homeowners' claim filed in the previous six months, and no currently open homeowners' claim.
5. Continue to the sample date/time selector and show the simulated confirmation.
6. Explain the live operational handoff: an NPP salesperson manually assigns the appointment to a field technician before calling the homeowner to confirm the date/time.
7. Repeat with one or more disqualification examples to show that the calendar is withheld and the user sees an explanation.
8. Briefly compare the preventative inspection and repairs/maintenance pages, highlighting how the ad message and landing-page intent stay aligned.

## Qualification logic agreed for the demo

- Residential property only.
- The person completing the form must be the homeowner. Power of attorney alone does not qualify.
- Check ZIP membership against NPP's combined approved service-area ZIP list. Do not disqualify based on which technician's territory the ZIP belongs to.
- No homeowners' insurance claim filed within the last six months.
- No currently open homeowners' insurance claim.
- Do not require the homeowner to identify visible storm damage; roof damage may not be visible from the ground.

## Safety and scope

- This is a simulated demonstration, not a live lead-generation campaign.
- No lead information is sent to NPP's GoHighLevel account.
- No real contact is created, no calendar slot is reserved, and no email/SMS/WhatsApp/customer or salesperson notification is sent.
- Do not enter real homeowner personal information during the demonstration. Use fictional sample details only.
- Do not describe the sample booking as a confirmed NPP appointment.
- Testimonials or review placeholders must be replaced with NPP-approved, verifiable reviews before public advertising.
- Before launching paid campaigns, David/NPP must approve service areas, qualification criteria, copy, creative, budget, tracking, and the actual GoHighLevel workflow.
