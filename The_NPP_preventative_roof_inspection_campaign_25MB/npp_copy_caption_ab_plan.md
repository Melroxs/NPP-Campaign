# NPP Preventative Roof Inspection Campaign

## Core conversion strategy

Position the inspection as a practical way to understand the visible condition of the roof and decide what deserves attention. The campaign should reduce uncertainty rather than create fear. Every asset should lead to one action: **Schedule a Roof Inspection**.

The most credible promise is not that an inspection prevents every problem. It is that NPP can look at the property, identify visible wear or maintenance concerns, explain what was found, and help the homeowner plan next steps.

## Recommended copy system

### Primary campaign line

**Your roof protects everything beneath it. Give it the attention it deserves.**

### Hook variants

**A — Protection:** Your roof protects everything beneath it.

**B — Early attention:** Don’t wait for a leak to check your roof.

### Supporting lines

- Identify visible wear and maintenance needs early.
- Review practical next steps with a local professional.
- Plan with information—not guesswork from the driveway.

### CTA variants

- Book Your Roof Inspection
- Get Started

Use one CTA per ad set. Do not rotate CTA wording inside the initial test, because that would make the aspect-ratio comparison less clean.

## A/B test plan: 4:5 versus 1:1

### Objective

Determine whether the 4:5 or 1:1 static format produces more qualified roof-inspection inquiries at an acceptable cost, while separating creative-format effects from audience, placement, copy, and delivery differences.

### Test design

Run a controlled split test with two cells:

- **Cell A:** the three NPP static ads in 4:5 format.
- **Cell B:** the matching three NPP static ads in 1:1 format.

Keep the concept order, headline, supporting text, logo treatment, caption, headline field, CTA button, audience, optimization event, budget split, bid strategy, landing page, and tracking parameters identical. Use the same campaign and ad-set structure where the platform permits a true split test. If using separate ad sets, name them clearly and lock budget allocation at 50/50.

Run both formats in the same eligible feed placements. Do not compare one format mostly in Instagram Feed against the other mostly in Facebook Feed. Exclude Stories and Reels from this static-format test unless both formats are explicitly eligible and delivered evenly.

### Suggested test window

Allow at least 7 full days and preferably 10–14 days. Avoid making decisions from the first 24–48 hours unless there is a tracking or policy problem. Do not edit the ads during the test; edits can reset learning and compromise the comparison.

### Measurement hierarchy

**Primary business metric:** cost per qualified lead or cost per booked inspection.

**Secondary business metric:** qualified-lead rate, contact rate, and booked-inspection rate.

**Leading indicators:** link CTR, landing-page-view rate, landing-page conversion rate, and cost per landing-page view.

**Diagnostic metrics:** thumb-stop or 3-second view rate where available, frequency, CPM, and comments or negative feedback.

Judge the winner on qualified outcomes first. A higher CTR is not a win if it produces lower-quality inquiries.

### Decision rules

Select a format as the provisional winner when it has:

1. At least 50 landing-page views per cell, or the closest practical threshold for the available budget.
2. At least 20 conversion events per cell when possible; otherwise label the result directional.
3. A lower cost per qualified lead or booked inspection, not merely a lower click cost.
4. No material difference in lead quality, service-area fit, or contactability.

If results are inconclusive, keep the better-performing concept and test a new variable such as the hook or CTA. Do not repeatedly retest the same formats without enough conversion volume.

### Reporting template

Record spend, impressions, reach, frequency, CPM, link clicks, CTR, landing-page views, landing-page-view rate, leads, qualified leads, booked inspections, cost per lead, cost per qualified lead, and cost per booked inspection for each cell. Add a short qualitative note on comments, visual legibility, and whether the roof/property remains clear at mobile size.

### Tracking and operational checks

Use distinct UTMs for `format_4x5` and `format_1x1`. Confirm that the landing page works on mobile, the form or call CTA is connected before launch, and the thank-you or call event is recorded. The provided demo landing page previously stated that its form was not connected to the NPP CRM, so verify CRM integration and consent/privacy review before sending paid traffic.

## Launch recommendation

Start with the protection-led hook as the control because it is broad, emotionally clear, and non-alarmist. Use the early-warning hook as the strongest challenger. Keep the planning hook for a second test or retargeting audience. Maintain the same caption across the 4:5-versus-1:1 test so the result reflects format rather than copy differences.
